# Monitoring programme

| Control check | Cadence | Owner | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Delivery SLAs, failed orders and outage trends | Weekly | Operations | KPI report, incident tickets |
| Security incidents, customer complaints, access exceptions | Monthly | IT Security | Incident and access exception logs |
| Remediation tracker and overdue actions | Monthly until closed | GRC | Action log, owner updates |
| Sub-processors, hosting and retention | Quarterly | Privacy | Change notices, vendor attestation |
| Access-control sample and security questionnaire | Six-monthly | IT Security / GRC | Screenshots, sample review |
| Recovery walkthrough and alternate capacity | Six-monthly | Operations | Exercise report |
| Full risk/criticality refresh | Annually | GRC | Signed assessment |

## Out-of-cycle triggers
- Suspected or confirmed breach: immediate incident triage and risk reassessment.
- Acquisition or change of control: assess ownership, infrastructure and financial stability.
- New sub-processor, hosting region or material data use: review before change where contract/law requires.
- Lapsed attestation, serious vulnerability or failed access control: targeted reassessment.
- Major outage or recurring SLA failures: criticality and resilience reassessment.
- Major increase in revenue concentration: update criticality and fallback capacity.

## Governance
Maintain monitoring evidence, issue owner, dates, trend and closure criteria. Escalate missed critical controls to Head of Risk and COO. Cadences are proposed and must be ratified in the actual vendor governance programme.
