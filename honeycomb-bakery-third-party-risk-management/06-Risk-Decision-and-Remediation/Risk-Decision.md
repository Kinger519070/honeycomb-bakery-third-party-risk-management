# Proposed management decision and remediation

## Recommendation
**Time-limited conditional continuation for 30 days, NOT an approved acceptance.** Immediate removal could disrupt a material revenue channel, but continued unchecked exposure is unacceptable. Approval must be recorded by the COO or delegated executive risk owner, with Head of Risk recommendation and Legal/Privacy review. No sign-off is simulated as genuine.

## Conditions and gates
| Due | Requirement | Owner | Escalation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Day 7 | Confirm DPA, incident contacts, hosting, sub-processors and privileged access | Privacy / IT Security | COO + Legal if missing |
| Day 14 | Review security evidence, incident history, driver access and integration | GRC / IT Security | Head of Risk if deficient |
| Day 30 | Continuity walkthrough, alternative delivery plan and contract addendum | Operations / Legal | Executive committee if incomplete |

**Immediate stop/restrict trigger:** confirmed active compromise, unlawful processing, serious unresolved access failure or unacceptable operational risk. Reassess risk and activate contingency, rather than blindly continuing to day 30.

## Authority and appetite
All open risks must have accountable owners. Inherent score 16/25 is above the assumed 12/25 tolerance, therefore active treatment is mandatory. The proposed 30-day exception is not permanent acceptance; a decision above delegated authority escalates to the appropriate risk committee/Board.

## Exit criteria
Received and reviewed core evidence; signed applicable safeguards; tested fallback; tracked findings with evidence and accountable owners; residual scores recalculated only for validated controls; formal executive decision documented.
