# Evidence register and document conventions

`REQ-01` through `REQ-09`: requested but not received. `F-01` through `F-06`: scenario fact or evidence gap. `ACT-01` onward: proposed remediation actions. `DEC-01`: proposed management decision, unsigned.

Never label fictional screenshots, certificates, policies or interviews as genuine. If extending this portfolio with sample vendor responses, mark each **SYNTHETIC TRAINING EVIDENCE** and maintain a separate assessment of its sufficiency. No personal customer data should be added to this public repository.
