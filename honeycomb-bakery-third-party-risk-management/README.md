# Honeycomb Bakery | Third-Party Risk Management

**Portfolio case study | Simulated engagement | 14 November 2026**

> **Disclosure:** Honeycomb Bakery and its delivery partner are fictional training scenarios. All decisions, management roles, dates, evidence references, findings and approvals are illustrative. No vendor interview, security test, contract review or external assurance was actually conducted. Do not treat these artefacts as a real vendor audit.

## Business scenario

Honeycomb operates 12 stores (8 UK, 4 US), an online ordering platform and approximately 200 employees. A small delivery partner is already live and receives customer names, delivery addresses, telephone numbers and order contents through its own driver application and infrastructure. The partner handles a material share of online fulfilment. No assessment was completed before signature.

## Project objectives

1. Separate inherent risk (potential harm from processing and connectivity) from criticality (business consequence of supplier loss).
2. Design proportionate due diligence, with alternative evidence for a small supplier.
3. Document confirmed scenario facts, evidence gaps and proposed risk treatment without fabricating audit results.
4. Define contract safeguards, continuous monitoring, escalation and verified offboarding.
5. Present a defensible decision to an executive risk owner.

## Method and references

This is a risk-based exercise informed by ISO/IEC 27001, ISO/IEC 27002, ISO/IEC 27036, NIST CSF 2.0 and applicable data protection obligations (UK GDPR, EU GDPR where relevant, and applicable US state law). Framework references are guidance, **not** a claim of compliance or certification. Whether UK GDPR processor terms, international transfer rules, or US privacy statutes apply must be validated against actual data flows and legal roles.

## Portfolio integrity

Evidence IDs marked `REQ` are **requests, not received evidence**. `GAP` means **not provided in the scenario**, not proof that a control is absent. The proposed approval is **not signed**. Residual scores are intentionally not asserted without validated controls.

## What I would do next in a real engagement

Send the evidence request, interview vendor security/operations, verify contractual processor roles, test integration access, perform continuity walkthrough, update findings with received artefacts, calculate evidenced residual risk and seek documented executive approval.
