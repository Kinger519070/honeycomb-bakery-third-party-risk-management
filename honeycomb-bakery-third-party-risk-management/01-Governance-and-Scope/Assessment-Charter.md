# Assessment charter

**Case:** HCB-TPRM-001 | **Date:** 2026-11-14 | **Status:** retrospective assessment, supplier live.

## Scope
Partner-operated driver app, vendor infrastructure, customer contact/order data, Honeycomb ordering integration, onward processors, operational resilience, contract safeguards and exit readiness.

## Out of scope
Payment card processing (handled by a separate payment provider), a full code audit, formal penetration testing and certifications. These may be revisited if evidence or incidents justify it.

## Confirmed scenario facts
12 Honeycomb stores (8 UK/4 US); ~200 employees; online platform; vendor receives names, addresses, telephone numbers and order contents; partner uses own app/infrastructure; vendor small; material revenue dependency; no pre-contract assessment.

## Unknowns
Exact contract provisions, hosting locations, sub-processors, cross-border transfers, control implementation, incident history, data volumes, business continuity arrangements and vendor finances.

## RACI (proposed, not approved)
| Activity | Responsible | Accountable | Consulted |
|---|---|---|---|
| Vendor assessment | GRC Manager | Head of Risk | IT Security, Privacy, Operations |
| Operational contingency | Operations Manager | COO | Procurement, GRC |
| Data protection terms | Privacy Officer / Legal | Legal lead | IT Security |
| Risk exception | Head of Risk | COO within delegated limits | Privacy, Legal |
| Exit validation | GRC | COO | IT Security, Privacy, Operations |

**Risk appetite assumption:** no risk scored above 12/25 may remain untreated; every risk has an owner. This is a case-study assumption supplied in the wider Honeycomb scenario, not evidence of an approved corporate policy.
