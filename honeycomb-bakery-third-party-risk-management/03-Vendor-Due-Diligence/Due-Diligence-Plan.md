# Proportionate due diligence plan

**Approach:** accept concise policies, redacted screenshots, short live demonstrations, management attestations and recent test summaries. Do not mandate ISO 27001 certification or SOC 2 Type II solely because the supplier is critical. Require substantive proof of core controls.

| Evidence ID | Request | Purpose | If missing |
|---|---|---|---|
| REQ-01 | Recent security review or completed questionnaire, with critical remediation status | Baseline security governance | High assurance gap; offer focused control demonstration or scoped independent review |
| REQ-02 | 24-month incident/breach register or signed declaration | Incident history and response maturity | History unverified; signed declaration and incident-response interview |
| REQ-03 | Sub-processor list, data flows and hosting countries | Onward processing and transfer exposure | High privacy gap; do not authorise new onward sharing until clarified |
| REQ-04 | Privileged MFA, driver onboarding/offboarding and access-review samples | Access restriction and account lifecycle | High gap; verify live controls and require urgent remediation if absent |
| REQ-05 | Encryption, retention and deletion procedures | Confidentiality and data minimisation | Require configuration sample and deletion demonstration |
| REQ-06 | Incident response plan, contact tree and notification test | Rapid escalation | Require documented procedure and tabletop exercise |
| REQ-07 | BCP/DR summary and recovery walkthrough | Delivery continuity | Critical business gap; require fallback route and exercise |
| REQ-08 | Signed DPA, processing instructions and contract excerpts | Processor obligations and enforceability | Legal escalation; amend contract where necessary |
| REQ-09 | API integration diagram, authentication and logging samples | Secure platform connectivity | Technical review and least-privilege restriction |

**Evidence status:** all REQ items are requested / not received in this simulated case. None should be represented as reviewed.

**Evidence evaluation:** record artefact owner, date, scope, reviewer, sufficiency, exceptions and next action in the workbook. An attestation is not proof of effective operation without appropriate corroboration.
